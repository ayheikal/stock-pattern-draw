How the app suggests a status: for each sector it takes the median company's revenue and earnings-per-share growth (yearly, else the latest quarter), minus inflation, and the share of companies whose earnings beat inflation. Growth needs at least two of these clearly positive, Shallow at least two clearly negative; anything else is Normal. Many EGX companies don't report these figures to the data source, so check the coverage line on each card. Your own status, note and events (rate decisions, new laws, contracts, …) always win over the suggestion.
The conclusion combines that financial status with the sector's price trend: the share of its stocks above their 50- and 200-day averages and its 3-month move (weighted by company size). When both agree you get Favour or Avoid; when they disagree, Accumulate, Watch or Caution. It also flags an overbought or oversold sector (median RSI) and whether your upcoming events lean positive or negative. This app's track record finds chart signals on their own have had little edge, so treat the trend as timing and the financials as the case. Data comes from TradingView's public screener and may be delayed or incomplete. Not advice.
