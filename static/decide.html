    How the call is made. Inputs that decide: your own plan in My profile (for a stock you hold, its stop, target, view and size rules come first) and the buy checklist (the gate for any buy). Inputs that weigh: the rating (fundamentals × trend), fair value against peers (EGX only) and the market mood (EGX 30's trend and how many EGX stocks are above their 200-day average). Context only: the chart outlook, since the Track record shows it has no reliable edge, and the halal tag (but your own not halal tag blocks a buy).
    Not held: checklist Avoid, rating Sell or your not-halal tag → Don't buy. Checklist Wait or Not enough data → the same. Checklist Ready → each weighing input for counts +1, against −1: 2 or more is a Strong buy, 0 or 1 a Buy, below 0 Wait.
    Held: your plan says sell (stop or target hit, bearish view, too big) → do that. Rating Sell → Sell. Two or more against (checklist, rating, fair value, market, halal) → Reduce. Your plan says Add more and nothing is against → Add more. Otherwise Hold.
    Fundamentals can't be backtested (there's no history of past figures), so this is discipline, not a forecast. Not advice.
