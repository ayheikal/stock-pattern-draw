A long setup needs five things on the last completed bar. Trend: EMA 8 above EMA 20, both rising. VWAP: close above the session's volume-weighted average price. RSI: above 50. Volume: relative volume 1.5× or more. Entry: a close above the 20-bar high, or a close up after retesting VWAP / EMA 20. Stop and target come from ATR (1.5 × and 3 ×). Ranked by rules passed, then relative volume; click a row to open its hourly chart in a new tab. Quotes can be about 15 minutes late, and when the market is closed this is the last session's final bar. Not advice.

Do these rules make money?

The rules replayed over the intraday history of the watchlist and bar size chosen above. At every bar they see only what was known then. A signal buys the next bar's open with the ATR stop and target, and is out by the session's close; one trade at a time per stock. After costs takes off your fee from My profile plus 0.1% slippage, on the buy and on the sell. Every bar (no rules) is the same trade taken blindly: what the rules have to beat.
