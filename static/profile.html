My money & rules used to tell you how many shares to buy

A common rule is to lose at most 1–2% of your money on any one trade and 6% on all of them together, and keep any one stock under 20–25%. Capital is the money you put in: cash is what's left of it after what you paid for your holdings, plus realized gains and dividends.

Halal (Shariah) list

Paste the current members of the EGX 33 Shariah Index from EGX's website: codes like XXXX.CA, or the whole constituents table (ISINs are matched too). Stocks on it get a Halal tag on the chart, screener, market page and here. EGX reviews the list every six months, so paste the new one when it changes. A stock that isn't on the list may still be compliant (the index only holds the most liquid ones), so it stays untagged unless you tag it yourself from its chart.
